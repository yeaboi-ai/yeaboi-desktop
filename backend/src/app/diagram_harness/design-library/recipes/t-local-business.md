# Recipe T: Local Business

## Quick ref

- **When:** Trades, clinics, salons, repair shops, service providers, restaurants (if logistics-first not Recipe D).
- **Token budget:** ~14k (HARNESS + ergonomics + components + page-archetypes + a11y/perf + anti-similarity).
- **Files to read:** `09-ergonomics.md`, `04-components.md`, `07-page-archetypes.md`, `10-accessibility-performance.md`
- **Personality fit:** Primary — Warm/Human. Strong — Professional, Playful, Organic. Bad fit — Brutalist.

## Page flow intent

INFORMATION-FIRST. The user searched "plumber near me" or "salon [town name]." They need: what you do, where you are, how to contact you. In that order, immediately.

Core rhythm: `what + where + CTA hero → services list → real photos of work/space → reviews → hours + map + contact`.

No scroll animations. No preloaders. Sub-3-second load. Click-to-call on mobile. The entire homepage should answer every question without scrolling on desktop.

## Techniques

- Hero: what you do + where + CTA (phone/book/visit). Instantly clear.
- Google Maps embed or custom map for location
- Service list with clear pricing (if applicable)
- Real photos of the business, team, work (not stock)
- Reviews/testimonials from Google/Yelp
- Business hours prominently displayed
- Click-to-call phone number (mobile)
- Before/after gallery for visual services (hair, construction, cleaning)
- FAQ addressing common customer questions

## Font direction

Clean, readable, trustworthy. No experimental typography — clarity is the priority. One good sans-serif covers it.

## Color

Professional but warm. Derive from existing brand/logo colors. Avoid generic blue. Ensure high contrast for older demographics.

## Motion budget

MINIMAL. Users want information fast. No scroll reveals, no parallax. Smooth transitions only. Performance is critical — often viewed on mobile data.

## Anti-patterns

Slow loading (Core Web Vitals matter for local SEO). Hidden phone number. No mobile optimization. Generic stock photos. Auto-playing music/video.
