# Recipe P: Non-Profit / Cause

## Quick ref

- **When:** Charities, NGOs, activism, fundraising, advocacy campaigns.
- **Token budget:** ~16k (HARNESS + imagery + ergonomics + page-archetypes + motion + anti-similarity).
- **Files to read:** `06-imagery-texture.md`, `09-ergonomics.md`, `07-page-archetypes.md`, `05-motion.md`
- **Personality fit:** Primary — Warm/Human, Organic. Strong — Professional, Editorial, Bold. Bad fit — Brutalist.

## Page flow intent

Lead with IMPACT, not mission statements. Big numbers first, story second, ask last. The donation CTA is EARNED through emotional engagement, not demanded on arrival.

Core rhythm: `impact numbers hero → scrollytelling narrative → real photography → progress/goals → CTA`.

The scrollytelling section is the heart — a sticky visualization that changes as the user scrolls through the story.

## Techniques

- Impact numbers as hero (metric cards at massive scale, monospace)
- Authentic photography — real people, real situations (not stock)
- Scrollytelling for impact story (Pudding pattern: sticky graphic + text scroll)
- Progress bars for fundraising goals
- Donation CTA persistent but not aggressive
- Split-text reveals for mission statement
- Before/after comparisons via scroll position
- Partner/supporter logo bar

## Font direction

Humanist sans (warm, approachable) + serif for editorial/impact statements. Avoid corporate coldness.

## Color

Warm, trustworthy. Earth tones, warm whites, muted accent. NOT corporate blue. Color should feel human, not institutional.

## Motion budget

MEDIUM. Scrollytelling is the star. Reveal animations for impact. Nothing flashy — sincerity over spectacle.

## Anti-patterns

Guilt-driven imagery. Generic "donate now" popups. Cold corporate blue palette. Stock photos of handshakes.
