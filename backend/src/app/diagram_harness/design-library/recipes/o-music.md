# Recipe O: Music / Artist

## Quick ref

- **When:** Musicians, bands, labels, album releases, artist portfolios.
- **Token budget:** ~15k (HARNESS + motion + color + imagery + typography + anti-similarity).
- **Files to read:** `05-motion.md`, `02-color.md`, `06-imagery-texture.md`, `01-typography.md`
- **Personality fit:** Primary — Brutalist, Bold. Strong — Playful, Editorial.

## Page flow intent

The page IS the album experience. Full-screen visuals, audio integration, minimal navigation. Think: album cover brought to life, not an artist biography.

Core rhythm: `immersive visual → discography horizontal scroll → tour dates → embedded player persists across everything`.

## Techniques

- Full-screen album art or artist photography as background
- WebGL/Canvas audio-reactive visuals if interactive
- Custom cursor reflecting artist identity
- Horizontal discography scroll
- Embedded audio player (not just Spotify embed)
- Tour dates as styled list with clip-path hover (Springs Estate pattern)
- Video background for hero
- Grain/noise overlay heavy (10-15% opacity — more than typical)

## Font direction

Genre-dependent. Hip-hop = bold brutalist. Classical = refined serif. Electronic = geometric/futuristic. The font IS the genre signal.

## Color

Album-art-driven. Extract palette from current release. Dark base always works. Consider pure black for maximum photography impact.

## Motion budget

VERY HIGH. This is expression, not information. Audio-reactive, parallax, custom cursors, transitions — go for it.

## Anti-patterns

Linktree-style link list. Generic bio page. Stock music imagery.
